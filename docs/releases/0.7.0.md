# PiSpark 0.7.0

## Faster to open, and replies read better as they arrive

- **A lighter first load.** PiSpark downloads about a fifth less before it shows anything: Agents, Settings and Archived load when you first open them, math is downloaded once instead of twice, and the styles are smaller. On a phone or a remote connection it opens sooner.
- **Replies look right while they're written.** Math shows as math, not as dollar signs, before its end arrives; ~~strikethrough~~ and _emphasis_ show as they're typed; a half-written image waits until its address is whole; and a line of dashes no longer flashes the line above as a heading.
- **Background output keeps up.** The output of a busy background process stays current in its view, starting from its newest lines, and a process printing a lot no longer slows the rest of PiSpark.

## A steadier live connection

- **"Live" means live.** If the connection drops, for example when a laptop sleeps or Wi-Fi goes, PiSpark notices within about 35 seconds and reconnects, instead of showing "Live" for a minute or more while nothing can arrive.
- **Less traffic while a reply is written.** An open session no longer reconnects every minute and receives the reply so far all over again.
- **Gentler after a restart.** Open tabs and phones reconnect a moment apart, not all at once.

## Sessions that hand work to other sessions

A session can now split a job across helper sessions and bring the results back in one reply, so you follow one conversation and not several.

- **Less waiting, fewer messages.** A session starts its helpers, waits for them and answers you in one go. Before, every helper reported back on its own and the session took a turn for each report.
- **The conversation shows its own work.** Each helper is one line where it was started. The line says whether it is working, has a question or has answered, and holds its answer. "Started here" in Details lists everything the conversation has going: sessions, processes and schedules.
- **A quieter session list.** Helper sessions no longer appear in the list. The conversation that started them shows a mark and a count while they work.
- **Nothing piles up.** A helper is put away once it has answered. Opening its line brings it back. A session keeps a helper around only when it means to ask it more.
- **Questions reach you only when they must.** A helper's question goes to the session that started it. You see "Needs you" only when that session cannot decide, and you answer by replying there.
- **You can step in.** Open a helper while it works to tell it something or to stop it. The session that started it is told what you did.
- **Background is one list.** Everything that runs or is scheduled across PiSpark, each row naming the conversation it belongs to.

### If you build agents or extensions

- **The tools have new names.** A session now uses `pispark_start_session`, `pispark_start_process`, `pispark_schedule`, `pispark_send`, `pispark_wait`, `pispark_look`, `pispark_stop`, `pispark_raise`, `pispark_note` and `pispark_self`. `pispark_session`, `pispark_process` and `pispark_report` are gone: an agent that switches tools on or off by name needs the new names.
- **A helper starts from the Default agent** and that agent's model, unless the session that starts it names another agent or model. It no longer copies the model of the session that started it.
- **Extensions can run their own workflows.** An extension can start, wait for and stop sessions and processes in code, hear what reaches its session before the agent does, and pass data both ways. "Sessions and processes from code" in `docs/extensions.md` shows how.
