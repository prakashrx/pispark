# Discover the charter

A charter is short: four headings, one page at most. It's the person's intent, not a plan.

1. **Find out first.** Look at what the place already says about itself: its README, any vision or goals documents, recent history, how it's organised. Draft what you can from that.
2. **Ask only what only the person can answer.** That's usually:
   - what this is for and for whom,
   - what good looks like to them,
   - what must never happen.

   Ask in one short message, with your draft so they can correct rather than write. Don't ask what you could find out yourself. Ask only when finding out failed or left it ambiguous.
3. **Record what they confirm** with `mind_record` (`origin: "told"`, quoting their words), one item per point, in the right section: `purpose`, `for-whom`, `good`, `off-limits`.
4. **Point, don't copy.** If the place already holds a vision or goals document, the charter can say so in one line instead of repeating it.

Rules about how you should behave toward the person ("ask before X", "just do Y") go in the contract, not the charter.
